Reorder Radar vs Stocky

Last updated: October 2026

Shopify switched Stocky off on August 31, 2026. If you used it to decide what to reorder, here's how Reorder Radar compares. We build Reorder Radar, so we'll also say where it isn't a replacement.

What happened to Stocky? Shopify's guide says that as of August 31, 2026, Stocky is no longer available. You have read-only access to export your data for at least 90 days after that date. Shopify also notes that suppliers can't be exported from Stocky, and that historical purchase orders can't be imported into Shopify. Read Shopify's transition guide.

The short version

Reorder Radar replaces Stocky's “what should I reorder, and when?” job. It forecasts from your Shopify sales and ranks products by how much profit you'd lose if they ran out.

It doesn't replace Stocky's purchasing workflow. There are no purchase orders, stock transfers or stocktakes. If you need those, use a dedicated purchasing or inventory tool, or Shopify's own purchase orders and transfers. Reorder Radar can run alongside them.

How Reorder Radar decides what to reorder

For each product, Reorder Radar works out a daily sales velocity from your Shopify orders. Recent days are weighted more heavily, so a product that is speeding up or slowing down shows it quickly. From that velocity, your current stock, your lead time and your safety stock, it calculates:

  • Days of stock left and the projected stockout date
  • An order-by date, or Order Now when you're already late
  • A reorder quantity that covers lead time plus safety stock
  • Profit at risk: the margin you'd lose on the days you'd be out of stock

Products that need ordering within the next week appear under Reorder Alerts, sorted by profit at risk. Everything else is under Monitoring. Each forecast is labelled with a confidence level based on how many sales it's built on.

Moving from Stocky: a checklist

  • Export what you can now. Download your purchase order reports, stocktakes and cost data from Stocky's reports while the read-only window is open.
  • Note your vendor lead times. In Stocky they're set per vendor, and Shopify says suppliers can't be exported. Write them down or take a screenshot.
  • Install Reorder Radar. It syncs your products, stock levels and recent orders from Shopify, so there's no history to import.
  • Set your defaults. In Settings, set a default lead time, margin and safety stock (in days). Then override the lead time or margin on any product whose supplier is slower or faster.

When Reorder Radar isn't the right fit

  • You need to create, send and receive purchase orders.
  • You need reorder recommendations for each warehouse or store location.
  • You rely on stock transfers or formal stocktakes.

If that's you, choose a full inventory platform. You can still use Reorder Radar alongside it for a ranked weekly reorder list.

Questions

Does Reorder Radar change my inventory?

No. It only has read access to your products, orders and inventory. It never writes to your Shopify store.

How much does it cost?

The Free plan covers your top 50 products, ranked by profit at risk. Pro is $49/month for unlimited products and the weekly email digest.
